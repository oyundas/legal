Privacy Policy

Summary

Playfellow (Oyundaş) is built to keep your shared moments private. Content you share with your connection is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it leaves it; the Developer cannot read it. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between games (never during play). Plus and Premium are ad-free. The Developer uses no analytics tools and never sells data (data that Google processes for ads is described in the Ads section).

Data controller

Playfellow is developed and provided by Egemen Enis Ertaş, an individual developer (in these documents, "the Developer"). The Developer is the controller of your personal data. This policy explains what the app processes, why, and your rights. The KVKK notice also contains the information required by Turkish law.

What the app processes

• Your display name and the relationship type you choose. The name is shared, encrypted, only with your connection; your opponent in Social does not see it.
• Game answers, notes, the shared list, mood entries and shared profile fields. They are encrypted on your device with your connection's key and stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Only the members of your connection can read them.
• Technical records needed to connect devices: a pseudonymous iCloud user identifier (a random identifier Apple generates for this app that contains no name or contact details), a lookup identifier derived one-way from your invite code (never the code itself), relationship type, group size and creation time.
• Your plan level: if you subscribe, an Apple-signed proof of your plan is shared, encrypted, with your connection. Payment details never reach the Developer.
• Support requests and player reports you send by email: your email address and what you write. They are used only to answer your request.
• Consent record: the version of the documents you accepted, which consents you gave, the date, and the app language and version. It is stored with your pseudonymous iCloud identifier in a record that only you and the Developer can read.
• Notification permission: if you allow it, Apple delivers notifications; your contact details never reach the Developer.

Social play

Social matches you through Apple's Game Center with someone who wants to play at the same moment. To be matched with real players you need to be signed in to Game Center on your device; your opponent sees your Game Center nickname, and your name in the app is not shared. Game Center is Apple's own service and is subject to Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/. During the game your answers travel only to your opponent through Game Center's infrastructure, end-to-end encrypted with a key created for that game. The Developer keeps no Social records on its servers. Inappropriate names are hidden and your opponent's free-text answers are not shown. If nobody is available, or if you are not signed in to Game Center, you play against a computer player that runs entirely on your device; the computer player is marked in the game. You can block a player; the Game Center player ID of a blocked player is stored only on your device. If you report a player, your own email app opens and the report reaches the Developer by email. The number of Social games you played today is stored only on your device.

What stays on your device

Your photo, language and appearance choices, Love Language result, recent game results and rewards are stored only on your device.

Web pages and invite link

These legal texts and the invite link page (oyundas.github.io) are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open these pages, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser information under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. The invite code sits after the “#” sign in the link, and browsers do not send that part to the server, so the code never reaches GitHub or the Developer and is read only on your device. The pages use no cookies or analytics tools.

Ads

In the free version the app uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) to show an occasional full-screen ad after a game and, if you choose, a rewarded video to try a locked game once (a limited number of times per day). Subscribers see no ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and iOS asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are not personalised. When an ad is shown, Google may process your IP address (approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interaction data and the ad SDK's crash and performance data in order to deliver, measure and analyse ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Your content, names and answers are never shared with Google.

Data is processed to perform the contract with you (providing the app), to comply with legal obligations, to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (the consent record), for the legitimate interest in security and preventing abuse and, where required, on the basis of your consent (for example transfers abroad and personalised ads). You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

International transfers

Apple and Google operate servers outside Turkey and outside your country. These countries may not offer the same level of data protection as Turkey or your country, and there may be risks such as access to data by foreign public authorities. By accepting in the app you explicitly consent to these transfers, aware of these risks. Apple and Google apply their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses; because your content is end-to-end encrypted, these companies cannot read it.

What is not done

No ads are shown during play or to subscribers; no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are used; no profiles are built about you; your content and personal data are not sold and are not shared with anyone except as described in this policy.

Retention and deletion

Unused invite codes expire after 24 hours. Profile > Leave Connection deletes your shared records from the cloud and removes the connection from your device; the code owner can close the connection for everyone or remove a member. The Developer keeps no Social records on its servers; reports and support requests received by email are deleted once resolved. The consent record is kept for the statutory limitation periods so that your consents can be proven; withdrawing consent is recorded in it. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Security

Shared content is protected with AES-256-GCM using a random 256-bit key created for each connection. While an invite is open, the key is stored in the cloud only in a form locked with your 12-character invite code (PBKDF2-SHA256, 200,000 rounds); when the invite expires, that copy is deleted. Keys stay in the iOS Keychain on your device. Plan proofs are verified against Apple's certificate chain. No system is completely secure; the Developer follows current best practice.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and their personal data is not knowingly processed. If you believe a child has used the app, contact the Developer, who will help delete the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing and complain to a data protection authority. Because most data is encrypted so that only you and your connection can read it, the fastest way to delete it is Leave Connection in the app. You can view and withdraw your consents in Profile > Settings & Support > My Consents. Withdrawing consent does not delete cloud data by itself; the “Withdraw Consent and Delete My Data” option on the same screen does both.
